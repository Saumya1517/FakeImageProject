Evaluation Methodology

These benchmarks were calculated by running the offline tool calculate_parameter.py over our test set of exactly 50 balanced images (25 real, 25 screen-retaken fakes).

Inference includes full image decoding, conversion of colour channels, resizing, ResNet18 forward embedding extraction, LBP and GLCM hand-crafted texture evaluation, and SVM classification.
